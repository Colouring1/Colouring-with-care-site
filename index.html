Why colouring, why memory

Colouring With Care began with my own family — sitting with a relative living with dementia, looking for something we could still do together. A blank colouring page didn't always work. But one drawn from a 1950s kitchen, or a jukebox, or a seaside deckchair — that opened a conversation.

Every page is drawn to be recognisable, not abstract: familiar rooms, familiar objects, familiar decades. They're designed to be easy to hold and colour, and to give carers and family an easy way in — something to talk about, not just something to fill in.
